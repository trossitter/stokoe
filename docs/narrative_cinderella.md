# Cinderella — Stokoe Notation Passage
## Gloss-level ASL encoding

**Format:** `[Stokoe ASCII notation]` / ENGLISH GLOSS / *parameter description*

Signs marked ✓ are from our verified training vocabulary (notation.ts).
Signs marked ~ are approximated from the Stokoe symbol table; accurate at the parameter level, not sourced from a verified ASL corpus document.

**Stokoe notation order:** Tab – Dez – Sig (location, handshape, movement)

### The Passage

*"A girl works all day. She goes to a ball. A prince finds her glass slipper. At midnight, everything changes. She is happy."*

### Reading Note

Like the Red Riding Hood passage, this extends the Goldilocks corpus precedent — demonstrating that Stokoe notation is a productive system capable of encoding novel narrative, not just a fixed dictionary. The ~ signs use the corpus symbols (`÷`, `×`, `@`, `z~`, `>`, `v`) identified from the Goldilocks passage, applied to new sign configurations.

The point: the notation system is generative. Stokoe designed it to describe any sign, not just the ones he had already seen. Our system uses that same generativity — and so can our learners, once they understand the framework.
